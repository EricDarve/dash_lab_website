Søren works at the intersection of AI and scientific workflows, leveraging
machine learning and agentic frameworks to accelerate and adaptively
automate existing pipelines for uncertainty quantification and optimization.

He is a former member of the Tchelepi research group at Stanford Energy
Science & Engineering, where he worked on accelerating risk assessment of
induced seismicity in subsurface problems through multi-fidelity Monte
Carlo-based frameworks combining physics-based simulation in GEOS with
data-driven methods including graph neural networks.

He has subject-matter expertise in uncertainty quantification, machine
learning (including operator learning and graph neural networks), and
multi-physics modeling, and has published 10+ journal papers in high-impact
venues spanning computational physics and numerical methods.
