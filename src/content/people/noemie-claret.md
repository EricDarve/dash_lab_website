Noémie is a first-year PhD student at Stanford ICME, working in
collaboration with the LCLS at SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory. She
works on high-throughput compression and signal processing algorithms
for crystallography, and is co-advised by Eric Darve and Frederic
Poitevin. She has a background in mathematics and her interests lie
broadly at the intersection of statistics, optimization, and scientific
machine learning.
